## Discussion 2: choose the first user journey

Recommended starting journey:

```text
An individual pastes bill text or forwards an appointment message.
The assistant extracts important details and asks about uncertainties.
The user confirms the obligation and reminder preference.
The assistant tracks the obligation and records completion with evidence.
The user can optionally share that specific obligation with an invited adult.
```

Discuss the following:

1. Which group can the team actually recruit: working renters, salaried workers, shift workers, or another defined group?
2. Which recurring obligation has enough value to justify repeat use?
3. What must the assistant do beyond a normal reminder to be useful?
4. Which steps may require the user's confirmation or completion in another service?
5. What information may be shared with a family member, and what remains private?
