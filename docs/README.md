## Start here

1. Use the [7 November kickoff agenda](meetings/2026-11-07-kickoff.md) to discuss scope, responsibilities, and open decisions.
2. Review the [governance charter](governance/project-charter.md) to assign owners and approve how the team will work.
3. Review the [timeline and development phases](planning/timeline-and-development-phases.md) against actual availability.
4. Consult the [business and architecture research brief](research/business-and-architecture-brief.md) for the broader evidence and product vision.

## Document status and relationship

| Folder | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `research/` | Business, competitor, feasibility, and architecture research | Research snapshot dated 6 November 2026; proposal, not implementation approval |
| `meetings/` | Dated agendas, discussion records, decisions, and actions | 7 November file is a prepared agenda awaiting actual outcomes |
| `governance/` | Charter, roles, authority, acceptance, and change control | Draft for team approval; names remain unassigned |
| `planning/` | Delivery phases, estimates, dependencies, and milestones | Draft using confirmed three-person capacity and 30 April 2027 deadline |

The research brief describes the **long-term product vision**. The newer charter and timeline propose a **smaller evaluated prototype** based on three members working 3–4 hours each per week. Do not interpret the research feature list as a commitment to deliver every capability by April.

## Updating the documentation

- Add future meeting records as `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
- Record approval dates and versions when the team adopts a baseline.
- Update cross-references when moving or renaming a file.
- Keep source citations and clearly label estimates, hypotheses, and confirmed decisions.
- Update this guide when adding a major document.

The architecture and timeline diagrams use fenced Mermaid blocks for GitHub rendering; their accompanying text and tables carry the substantive details.
