## 2. Risks more than one member raised

1. **Feature parity with three existing products (all three members).** The confirmed scope, LINE reminders with Google Calendar, is what Abdul, Lamai and Cole already advertise, and they advertise more. Abdul has a free tier and annual plans from ฿990, which undercuts ฿99 a month.
2. **No demand evidence (all three).** No interviews, no waitlist, no payment, no prototype. Nobody has shown that anyone wants this or will pay.
3. **The differentiation is invisible or unproven (all three).** The trust rules only show when something goes wrong, so they do not sell the product on first use. Nobody has shown that "externally verified done" can be delivered for any biller.
4. **No distribution (Offers, Monopoly).** No channel tested, acquisition cost unknown, and no route to the first 100 users, against an illustrative break-even of about 4,800 paying users.
5. **The price leaves no room (all three).** Contribution of ฿12–62 per user cannot carry human help, a guarantee or support. Variable cost is unmeasured.
6. **The target is too broad (Offers, Monopoly).** "Office workers in Bangkok" is the whole market, not a group that can be won.
7. **The pain may be a nuisance, not a cost (Offers, Monopoly).** Free substitutes already cover reminders. Nobody has put a baht figure on a missed obligation.

Raised by one member only: the first five minutes is undesigned, and the handoffs between LINE, the model, Google Calendar and the user can fail silently (Product).

## 3. Conditions checklist

- [ ] **One narrow, countable buyer** reachable through a channel the team already has, replacing "office workers in Bangkok" (Offers, Monopoly). Tested by `/founder-consumer`.
- [ ] **15 interviews in that group**, recording what missed obligations cost them last year and what the three competitors leave unsolved; collect their real bills (all three). Real-world work; `/founder-consumer` only simulates it.
- [ ] **One thing, in one clause**: "forward a bill, never miss it", sold as an outcome such as "never pay a late fee again" (Offers, Product). `/founder-offer`.
- [ ] **One obligation type done far better than any competitor**, shown side by side against Abdul on the same task (Monopoly). `/founder-competitors`.
- [ ] **Make the trust visible**: show the source snippet beside every extracted date and amount in the confirmation message (Product).
- [ ] **Find out whether verified completion can be delivered** for at least one bill type, and whether people would pay for it (Offers, Monopoly).
- [ ] **Measure the first five minutes**: extraction accuracy on real Thai bills and time to first confirmed obligation, with a target set in the proposal and reported in April (Product).
- [ ] **Design the failure states** (uncertain field, muted push, failed calendar write) inside the committed scope (Product).
- [ ] **A real price signal**: a pre-sale or paid waitlist at ฿99 and ฿199, counting payments (Offers); at minimum a stated price or waitlist sign-up in the April evaluation (Product). `/founder-pricing`.
- [ ] **One tested channel**: 50 sign-ups at a recorded cost, at least 10 paying any real price (Offers, Monopoly). `/founder-marketing`, `/founder-launch`.
- [ ] **Measured variable cost per active user**, so the price is known to leave a margin (Offers, Monopoly). `/founder-cfo`.
- [ ] **A guarantee the margin can carry**, for example a refunded month if the product misses a confirmed deadline through its own fault (Offers). `/founder-offer`, `/founder-cfo`.
- [ ] **Drop the spending assistant** from the proposal for now (Offers, Product).
- [ ] **Disputed: family or group sharing.** See below.

### Where the members disagree

- **Family sharing.** The Monopoly member wants a household or group feature in scope, because it is the only mechanism here where each user brings another. The Product member wants everything beyond the one thing cut. The project deferred family sharing on 17 November. This is unresolved.
- **Cut or defer.** The Product member wants booking, personalities and the other candidates removed from the proposal entirely. The Offers member only asks that the spending assistant be dropped for now.
- **Whether it is fundable at all.** The Product member would fund on five conditions the team can meet inside the senior project. The other two want paying users and a tested channel first, which the planned April evaluation does not produce.

## 4. The strongest version the board can see

This differs from what was pitched. The pitch is a general LINE assistant for office workers with reminders, calendar, spending, booking and personalities. The strongest version is much narrower: one named group the team can reach directly, one bill type that costs that group real money when missed, and one promise, "forward the bill and never pay a late fee on it". The confirmation message shows the source beside every extracted date and amount, so the trust is visible on first use, and the product is demonstrably better than Abdul on that single task. The April evaluation would then measure extraction accuracy on real bills, time to first confirmed obligation, and at least one real demand signal. Whether a household feature belongs in that version is the open disagreement: it is the only route to growth the board identified, and it is also more scope.
