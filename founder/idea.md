# f-abdul (working name; not final)

- What it is: an AI personal assistant inside LINE that turns a forwarded bill, document or appointment message into a tracked obligation, reminds the user through LINE and their calendar, and records honestly whether it was completed.
- Who it is for: office workers and other individual adults in Thailand (Bangkok first) who manage their own bills, rent, renewals and appointments.
- What it sells, at what price: a monthly subscription. Price is an untested hypothesis from the project's research brief: free tier, Personal at ฿79 or ฿99/month. No price has been tested with buyers.
- Where and how: online, delivered entirely through a LINE Official Account (with a small embedded web page for account linking). No acquisition channel has been tested; candidates are communities of working adults and user referrals.
- Budget and constraints: three university students, 3–4 hours each per week (9–12 team-hours/week). No approved budget. Senior project: proposal due 22 Nov 2026, evaluated prototype due 30 Apr 2027. No code exists yet.
