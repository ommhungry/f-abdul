# Competitors and the gap: f-abdul (working name)

Researched 17 November 2026 from public pages only. Every price and feature below is what the vendor's own page advertises; none was tested. Data: [competitors.csv](competitors.csv).

**Limits of this research**

- LINE Official Accounts have no store listing, so there are no ratings or review counts for the direct competitors.
- Four searches for independent user reviews of Abdul, Lamai and the other LINE assistants (Pantip, Facebook, Threads, complaint keywords) returned only the vendors' own pages. Step 3 is therefore thin, and no complaint theme reaches three reviews.
- Not opened: Toki's and MeowJot's official pages, any LINE friend counts, and the assistants themselves.

## 5. The gap

**For the confirmed scope there is no gap.** Text capture, reminders and Google Calendar are advertised by Abdul, Lamai, YES BOSS! and Cole, with free tiers. Abdul's own headline already names card bills ("ค่าบัตร").

**The proposed additions are also taken:**

- **Spending and slips:** YES BOSS! advertises income and expense tracking with unlimited slip reading at ฿199, and a free tier with three wallets. MeowJot reads bank slips automatically and MAKE has a free monthly Expense Summary with 310k ratings; both come from a bank.
- **Personalities:** Abdul lists selectable habits, renaming and gender choice as coming soon.
- **Renewals:** eMemo is built around exactly this, with 30, 7 and 1-day reminders.

**What nobody advertises:**

1. **Bill in, obligation out.** No page describes forwarding a bill photo or PDF and getting back the due date and amount with the source shown beside each field. eMemo says it does not ask for document photos ("ไม่ขอรูปเอกสาร") and lists photo input as coming soon. Cole and Lamai handle files generally; YES BOSS! reads slips for expense logging, which is after payment, not before the due date.
2. **Honest completion.** No page distinguishes "I said I paid" from "payment evidence attached" or from an externally verified payment.

This gap is real on paper and narrow. It rests on what vendors advertise, not on what users complain about, because no complaints could be found. It matches the board's "one thing": forward a bill, never miss it. Whether anyone wants it enough to switch or pay is untested.

## 6. The threat

- **Abdul** could copy fastest: it already targets bills in its headline, has reminders, memory, file storage and a paid base, and would need only bill-photo extraction.
- **eMemo** has already announced photo input and document summaries as coming soon, in the same renewals niche.
- **YES BOSS!** already reads slips and receipts, so reading a bill before payment is a small step.
- **Banks and billers** own the verified-payment data. If "verified done" matters, they can provide it and an outside assistant cannot, short of the user attaching a slip.

A copy of f-abdul's feature takes one of these teams weeks. Nothing found here suggests a technical barrier.

### What this adds up to

Gaps 1 to 5 are one product, not five features: **the whole life of a bill, from arrival to proof of payment.** Every competitor is a reminder or a secretary. A reminder ends at "it is time". None of them covers what happens before (reading the bill) or after (paying it and proving it).

```text
forward the bill  ->  due date + amount, source shown   (gap 1)
reminder arrives  ->  with the bill's own payment QR    (gap 2)
user pays         ->  sends the slip; amount is matched (gap 3)
any time          ->  "฿4,300 due before the 25th"      (gap 4)
next month        ->  "new bill? send it" for the amount (gap 5)
```

This is the board's "forward a bill, never miss it", extended to "and know it is paid". It also gives the team's existing differentiation claim something concrete: "verified done" becomes "slip attached and amount matched", which the team can build, where true external verification belongs to banks.

Gap 6 is the Monopoly member's growth mechanism in its smallest form (send one bill to a flatmate or parent). Family sharing is deferred, so it is recorded here and not recommended for April.

### What is unproven

- **Feasibility of gap 2.** Whether the QR or barcode printed on Thai bills can be cropped and re-sent at reminder time, and whether bank apps accept it from a saved image, has not been checked.
- **Feasibility of gap 3.** Reading the amount and date from a slip image is ordinary extraction. Confirming the slip is genuine needs a bank or third-party slip-verification service, which has not been researched; without it the status must stay "evidence attached", not "verified".
- **Demand.** No user has asked for any of this. The 15 interviews should ask how people pay bills today, what they do with the slip, and whether a missed bill ever cost them money.
- **Copy risk.** YES BOSS! already reads slips and eMemo has announced photo input; either could reach gaps 1 and 3 quickly.
- **Effort.** None of gaps 1 to 5 is estimated. Image extraction was "conditional" in the original scope and is not in the timeline's 113–160 h.
